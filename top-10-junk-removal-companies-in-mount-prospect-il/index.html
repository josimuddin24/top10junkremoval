FAQ

Junk removal in Mount Prospect: common questions

How much does junk removal cost in Mount Prospect, IL?

Most jobs fall between $100 - $600. Single items such as a couch or mattress typically cost $75 to $175, while a half-truck load runs about $250 to $450 and a full truckload $500 to $800 or more. Ask for a photo-based or on-site quote before work begins.

Can I get same-day junk removal in Mount Prospect?

Often, yes. Several companies on this list offer same-day or next-day pickup when you call before noon. Junk Robbers and local crews such as BeeZee Junk Removal are good first calls for urgent cleanouts.

What items will a junk removal company take?

Most haulers take furniture, mattresses, appliances, electronics, yard waste, construction debris and general clutter. Hazardous materials such as paint, chemicals, asbestos and medical waste are usually excluded, but a good crew will point you to the right disposal option.

Do I need to move my junk to the curb?

No. Full-service junk removal means the crew carries items out of the basement, attic, garage or apartment, loads the truck and sweeps up afterward. Curbside pickup is usually a lower-cost option if the items are already outside.
