Methodology

How we rank junk removal companies

Junk removal is a local business, so a company that shines in Chicago may not serve Springfield at all. We built this guide around five factors that predict a smooth job:

  • Coverage and responsiveness: confirmed service area, realistic arrival windows and same-day or next-day availability.
  • Service breadth: furniture, mattresses, appliances, e-waste, yard waste, construction debris, estate and hoarder cleanouts, and light demolition.
  • Pricing transparency: volume-based quotes, photo estimates and no surprise fees.
  • Reputation and trust: review volume and consistency, licensing and insurance, and clear communication.
  • Responsible disposal: donation and recycling practices that keep usable items out of landfill.

What junk removal costs in Illinois

Most Illinois jobs are priced by truck volume. A single item typically runs $75 to $175, a half-truck load $250 to $450, and a full truckload $500 to $800 or more. Heavy materials such as concrete, dirt and shingles are usually priced separately by weight.
